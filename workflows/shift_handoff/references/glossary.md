# Plant glossary

- **Andon** -- a call for help raised at a station; the line may stop until it is answered.
- **Jidoka** -- stop the process at the first sign of a defect; never pass a defect on.
- **Takt** -- the planned time per unit to meet demand.
- **OEE** -- overall equipment effectiveness (availability x performance x quality).
- **TL / GL** -- team leader / group leader.
- **PM** -- planned (preventive) maintenance.
- **Containment** -- parts quarantined or extra inspection while a problem is open.
- **5-Why / RCA** -- root-cause analysis; see `skills/five-whys-rca.md`.
